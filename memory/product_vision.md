# Kriya - Product Vision

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-14

## Core Idea

Kriya is an **event staffing and brand activation platform** connecting young people (college students, ages 18-30) with short-term event gigs. Companies and event organizers post gigs; students and young professionals pick them up.

**NOT Urban Company.** We don't do home services, cleaning, plumbing, etc.

**Think:** Bangalore Tech Summit needs 50 registration desk staff → posts on Kriya → college students apply → get hired → earn money + build reputation.

## Name Meaning

"Kriya" (Sanskrit: क्रिया) means "action" or "deed" — reflecting the app's focus on getting things done through community trust.

## Target Market

### Gig Workers (Supply Side)
- **Primary:** College students (18-25)
- **Secondary:** Young professionals looking for side income (25-30)
- **Pilot:** Bangalore colleges initially

### Gig Posters (Demand Side)
- Event management companies
- Corporate event organizers
- Brand activation agencies
- Exhibition/trade show organizers
- Startups needing event support
- Marketing agencies

## Use Cases

1. **Event Staffing**
   - Registration/check-in desk
   - Ushering and crowd management
   - Badge printing and distribution
   - Information desk
   - VIP handling

2. **Brand Activation**
   - Booth staff at exhibitions
   - Product demonstrators
   - Brand ambassadors
   - Sampling and distribution
   - Lead collection

3. **Conference Support**
   - Session coordinators
   - AV/tech support runners
   - Hospitality staff
   - Social media coverage
   - Photography/videography assistants

4. **Promotional Work**
   - Flyer distribution
   - Mall activations
   - College campus promotions
   - Street teams

## Non-Negotiables

1. **Dual Identity by Default**
   - A student can work gigs AND post gigs (e.g., college fest coordinator)
   - No artificial role separation
   - UI adapts based on current activity context

2. **Momentum Cannot Be Bought**
   - Earned through genuine platform participation
   - Represents real-world reliability and contribution
   - Core anti-pay-to-win principle

3. **Trust > Distance**
   - Matching prioritizes trust signals over proximity
   - Past event performance, punctuality, communication matter most

4. **India-First Design**
   - INR as primary currency
   - UX assumptions for Indian market (mobile-first, data-conscious)
   - Phone number as primary identity (not email)
   - College ID verification

5. **Light Mode Only (Initial)**
   - Vibrant, high-contrast colors
   - Accessibility-first design
   - Dark mode deferred to v2

## Product Principles

- **Speed**: Quick gig posting, instant apply, fast hiring
- **Reliability**: Show up, do the job, build reputation
- **Fairness**: Dual reputation prevents exploitation on both sides
- **Transparency**: Clear pay, clear expectations, no surprises
- **Community**: Students help students get opportunities

## What We're NOT

- ❌ Home services (Urban Company)
- ❌ Delivery platform (Dunzo, Swiggy)
- ❌ Freelance marketplace (Upwork, Fiverr)
- ❌ Full-time job board (LinkedIn, Naukri)
- ❌ Household errands

## Success Metrics (Future)

- Gig completion rate
- Repeat hire rate (same worker hired again)
- Time to fill (how fast gigs get workers)
- Worker earnings per month
- Event organizer satisfaction
